First-Timer Guide

What Is Fantasy Football?

  • You pick a roster of real NFL players and earn points based on how they perform each Sunday.
  • Each week you go head-to-head against one other person in the league. Highest score wins that matchup.
  • The regular season runs Weeks 1 through 13. The top 8 teams make the playoffs (Weeks 14 through 16).
  • You do not need deep football knowledge to win. Consistency goes a long way.

Draft Day Tips

  • Load up on RBs and WRs early. They generate the majority of your weekly points.
  • Wait on QB and kicker. Depth at skill positions matters far more early in the draft.
  • Target a second RB and WR before drafting a Tight End.
  • Draft at least 2 RBs and 3 WRs in your first 7 picks to establish reliable depth.

Weekly Lineup Basics

  • Check your lineup weekly. Games lock Thursday night and Sunday morning.
  • Check injury reports before kickoff. Swap out players marked "Out" or "Questionable".
  • Watch bye weeks. Every team has one week off during the season. Never start a player on a bye week.
  • Always maintain a full starting lineup to prevent empty scoring slots.

Waivers & Free Agents

  • Waivers allow you to add unowned players off the free-agent pool each week.
  • Unclaimed players clear waivers every Wednesday morning.
  • Save high waiver priority for clear starters or breakout opportunities.

Scoring Overview

  • Passing TD: 4 pts  |  Rushing/Receiving TD: 6 pts
  • Every 10 rushing/receiving yards: 1 pt  |  Every 25 passing yards: 1 pt
  • Reception (PPR): 1 pt per catch. Rewards pass-catching receivers and backs.
  • Field goals score 3, 4, 5, or 6 points based on distance.
