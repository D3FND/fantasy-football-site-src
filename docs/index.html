The Weekly Manager Routine

  1. Tuesday by 11:59 PM PT: Submit Waiver Claims

    Review injuries from Sunday and Monday games, then submit claims for emerging free agents.

  2. Wednesday Morning: Waivers Process

    Unclaimed players become free agents between 12:00 AM and 3:00 AM PT. Pick up depth pieces for zero waiver-priority cost.

  3. Thursday by 5:00 PM PT: Thursday Night Football Lock

    Check whether you have active players playing tonight and set your lineup before the game locks.

  4. Saturday Evening: Official Injury Status Check

    Final practice designations are posted: Out, Doubtful, or Questionable.

  5. Sunday by 9:30 AM PT: Sunday Morning Kickoff Lock

    Official inactive lists release 90 minutes before kickoff. Ensure no “OUT” players remain in starting slots.

Essential Rules & Pro Tips for Beginners

  1. 1. The Golden Rule of the FLEX Spot

    Never start a Thursday night player in your FLEX slot. Put them in a dedicated RB or WR slot, leaving FLEX open for Sunday flexibility in case of late injuries.

  2. 2. Managing the Waiver Wire

    Volume is king. Target running backs with 15+ touches or wide receivers with 7+ targets per game. Save top waiver priority for clear replacement starters—not lateral depth.

  3. 3. Handling Bye Weeks

    Every NFL team has one scheduled off week. Don’t roster two kickers or two team defenses through a bye; stream a one-week replacement instead of dropping valuable running backs or wide receivers.

  4. 4. Navigating Questionable Players

    Questionable players may play, so monitor their status Sunday morning. For a late-afternoon or Monday game-time decision, keep an active backup available in the same late window.

  5. 5. Evaluating Matchups Over Projections

    Projected scores are estimates, not reality. Focus on player opportunity (targets and carries) and the opponent’s defensive ranking against that position.
